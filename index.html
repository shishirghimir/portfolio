About

Security engineer who ships.

I'm Shishir Ghimire — known online as NetaNix. I'm the founder & CEO of NetaNix Labs, where I design and build everything from a competitive CTF platform to the billing and workforce systems that keep businesses running.

My lane is the overlap of offense and engineering: I create CTF challenges, build forensic and recovery tooling, and then turn around and ship full-stack products. I'm a cybersecurity & ethical hacking student at Softwarica College (Coventry University), and I write up what I break on Medium.

Recent highlights: 5th place at HackAstra 2026, a Black Hat MEA 2026 CTF qualification, and a Top 1% global rank on TryHackMe.
