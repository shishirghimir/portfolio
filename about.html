About

Who I am.

Cybersecurity engineer, CTF creator, and founder of NetaNix Labs — breaking things, then building better ones.

I'm Shishir Ghimire, known online as NetaNix. I'm a cybersecurity & ethical-hacking student at Softwarica College of IT & E-Commerce (affiliated with Coventry University, UK), and the founder & CEO of NetaNix Labs — the lab where I turn security ideas into real products.

My work sits where offensive security meets software engineering. I create CTF challenges and run the NetaNix CTF platform; I build forensic and recovery tooling like MemVault and CrackVault; and I ship production systems — workforce management, cloud POS, trekking platforms — that businesses depend on every day.

I compete too. I placed 5th at HackAstra 2026, qualified for the Black Hat MEA 2026 CTF, and I'm in the Top 1% globally on TryHackMe. I'd call myself a creator first and a player second — though I do enjoy leading a scoreboard now and then.

Everything I break, I write up on Medium as @netanix96. Based in Kathmandu, Nepal — building for everywhere.
